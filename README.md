## 📊 Project Title: E-commerce Festive Sales Analysis

## 🎯 Business Objective:
Analyze the impact of festive seasons on e-commerce performance to optimize pricing, inventory planning, and return risk management.

---

## ⚙️ End-to-End Solution:

- **Python (Data Processing & Feature Engineering):**
  - Cleaned and transformed 9,000+ transaction records
  - Handled missing values and created key features like **Sales Value** and **Festival Category**

- **SQL (Data Validation & KPI Calculation):**
  - Wrote complex queries to validate data integrity
  - Calculated business KPIs such as **AOV (Average Order Value)**, **Return Rate**, and **Revenue Trends**

- **Power BI (Dashboard Development):**
  - Built an interactive 2-page dashboard
  - Visualized **Festival-wise Sales**, **Customer Segmentation**, and **Return Risk Analysis**
  - Enabled dynamic filtering using slicers (Festival, Category, Customer Type)

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights:

1. **Revenue Growth Opportunity:**
   - Festive seasons increase AOV by **3.7%**, indicating higher customer spending behavior

2. **Operational Risk:**
   - Return rates rise to **~10% during festivals**, increasing logistics and cost pressure

3. **Discount Optimization:**
   - Discounts beyond **30% show diminishing returns**, identifying a **sweet spot for profit maximization**

---

## 🚀 Business Impact:
- Helps businesses **optimize discount strategies**
- Supports **inventory planning during high-demand periods**
- Reduces **return-related losses through better forecasting**
